Fonts with really good quality (packages are Arch)

# Core Fonts ([sans-]serif & mono)
* [Liberation Fonts](https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts) (`ttf-liberation`)
  * Good core fonts to replace the "Big 3" while remaining metrically compatible
* [Libertinus](https://github.com/alerque/libertinus) (`otf-libertinus`)
  * Nicer replacement for Times New Roman than Liberation Serif
* [Noto Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/noto/) (`noto-fonts`)
  * Important fallback for every language

# Sans-Serif
* [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter) (`inter-font`)
* [DejaVu Sans](https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/) (`ttf-dejavu`)
* [Public Sans](https://public-sans.digital.gov/) (`otf-public-sans` <sup>AUR</sup>)
* [Source Sans](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans) (`adobe-source-sans-fonts`)


# Serif

* [Source Serif](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif) (`adobe-source-serif-fonts`)

* [EB Garamond](https://open-foundry.com/fonts/eb_garamond_regular) (`ebgaramond-otf` <sup>AUR</sup>)

# Monospaced

* [Commit Mono](https://commitmono.com/) (`ttf-commit-mono` <sup>AUR</sup>)
* [Iosevka](https://typeof.net/Iosevka/) (`ttc-iosevka`)
* [JetBrains Mono](https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/) (`ttf-jetbrains-mono`)
* [Hermit](https://pcaro.es/hermit/) (`otf-hermit`)
  * Interesting style
* [Cascadia Code](https://github.com/microsoft/cascadia-code) (`ttf-cascadia-code`)
  * Cool style, may not be as complete as others
* [Noto Sans Mono](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+Mono) (`noto-fonts`)
