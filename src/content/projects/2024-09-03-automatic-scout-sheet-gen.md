---
title: Automatic Scout Sheet Generator
pubDate: 2024-09-03
---

The Scout Sheet Generator is an automated tool that helps turn structured player data into clean line sheets for hockey scouts. Intended for youth hockey programs, it allows managers to quickly assemble sheets throughout the season by inputting a CSV, rather than manually assembling scouting documents in something like Microsoft Word. The generator reduces administrative overhead and ensures that every sheet throughout the season follows a clear structure, making it easier to compare players and share insights with those who count. By automating the repetitive parts of the scouting workflow, it allows evaluators to focus on what matters most: analyzing the game and identifying talent.

Currently, the generator is proprietary and private.
